## What makes Noah bail

Rarely active hatred — usually **boredom / disengagement**. Specific irritants:
- **Repetition that doesn't evolve** — mechanics or plots that stay the same (Spider-Man boss fights, late-season Office).
- **A steep, sprawling learning curve with no existing mental model** (CK3) — contrast Civ, which he already knows.
- **Myth retellings that break canon or characterization** (the PJ films, the Athena/Heracles book). As a Classicist, fidelity to the source matters; flag big liberties when recommending a retelling.
- **Gratuitous or off descriptions of characters** (The Magicians).
